Privacy note

This website belongs to Rajan Kumar V K, an independent management consultant based in Finland. This note explains what happens to the details you send through it.

What is collected

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, email address, organisation, phone number if you add it, the topic and your message. The website sets no cookies and uses no visitor tracking or analytics.

How it is used

Your details are used for one purpose: answering your enquiry and, if you wish, discussing a possible assignment. They are never sold or passed on for marketing.

Who handles it

The form is delivered by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, which forwards your message to my email inbox. If you choose WhatsApp, your conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to an assignment are deleted within twelve months. Correspondence belonging to an assignment is kept for as long as the law and the contract require.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to rajanvk56@outlook.com.
