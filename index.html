A problem solver who works from your numbers, not from opinion.

Most assignments begin where an organisation feels pressure but lacks clear figures: margins slipping, capacity sitting idle, energy bills climbing, or a technology pilot that never reached daily use.

My work locates where money and time are lost, prices each fix with its payback, implements the changes alongside the people who run the process, and installs the KPIs and dashboards that keep the improvement visible. The methods range from Lean Six Sigma, value stream mapping and statistical process control to Power BI, Python, predictive models and digital twins. I choose them by what the problem needs, never by fashion.

That practice rests on four degrees, doctoral research with 179 Finnish small and medium-sized companies on how technology investment turns into business and environmental results, and two years of business exploitation and technology transfer in a European industrial consortium. Earlier years as a production supervisor on a factory floor in Nepal keep the advice practical.

Worked examples you can open and test

Six live demonstrations of how I analyse a problem. The data are simulated or fictional; the methods and code are public on GitHub.

Business performance analysis

Margin recovery case

Sales grew 2.3% while EBITDA margin fell from 13.0% to 9.3%. A driver-based bridge separates price, material, cost-to-serve and fixed-cost effects, flags 29 loss-making accounts, and sizes five corrective actions worth about EUR 8.4 million.

Open the margin recovery case

Investment and operations analytics

Energy efficiency investment, evaluated

Hypothesis testing, difference-in-differences and regression isolate a true saving of 37.7 kWh per tonne from weather effects, and correct the payback from 1.8 to 2.6 years.

Open the analytics study

Predictive maintenance

Wind turbine digital twin

A physics-based twin of a 5 MW turbine detects bearing wear about 25 days before failure and values each intervention against a baseline without the twin.

Open the turbine twin

Asset management under budget limits

Bridge portfolio learning twin

A sensor, edge and cloud architecture ranks repair and renewal across an ageing bridge portfolio under a fixed budget: 52% fewer weight-limit bridge-years across 100 simulated portfolios.

Open the bridge twin

Logistics optimisation

Storage-minimised hydrogen system twin

A 720-hour simulation of electrolysis and trailer logistics. Rolling 48-hour optimisation reaches 100% service with 23% lower cost per kilogram and 92% less stationary storage than a fixed timetable.

Open the hydrogen twin
